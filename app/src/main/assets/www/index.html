EXPERIMENT PROTOCOL & METHODOLOGY
01
Check Sensor Hardware: Open Monitor and press START SENSORS. Verify sample rate (Hz) and whether your device exposes a hardware Gyroscope or Accelerometer only.
02
Calibrate Noise Floor: Go to Calibrate, hold the phone in your normal grip, and run the 4s Calibration to set a threshold above natural hand jitter.
03
Collect 4×10 Corner Taps: In Taps, keep your grip consistent. Tap the physical corner bezel/frame—not the touchscreen—10 times per corner.
04
Use Phantom Controller: Switch to Phantom and tap ENGAGE CONTROLLER. Physical taps trigger luminous corner ripples and L1/R1/L2/R2 controller readouts.
